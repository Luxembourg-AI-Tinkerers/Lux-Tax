# Luxembourg resident personal income tax — filing rules and 2025/2026 reforms

**Research date:** 8 November 2026  
**Scope:** Residents, tax years 2025 (filed in 2026) and 2026 rules that already affect 2025 returns or the 2026 filing season.  
**Question:** Who must file Form 100, on what timetable, through which channels, how joint assessment works, what changed in the 20 December 2024 / 2025–2026 budget laws, and whether the forms are really “100A–100L”.  
**Overall confidence:** High on filing obligation, deadline, channels, CIS, CIM, Bëllegen Akt, assiette thresholds and Form 100 structure (official ACD/guichet pages read today). Medium-high on the full 2025 barème table (ACD Mémorial A 590 cited by ACD and corroborated by three independent readings of the ACD tarif page). Medium on a few 2026 budget measures that are clear in the Ministry of Finance communiqué but whose LIR article numbers were not all opened in Legilux today.

---

## 1. Conclusion summary

Residents who are “imposés par voie d’assiette” must file **modèle 100**. For tax year 2025 the filing window is **7 April 2026 to 31 December 2026**; the legal deadline is **31 December of the year following the tax year** (guichet.public.lu, updated 7 April 2026). Salaried and pension residents who are **not** subject to assiette may regularise withholding with a **modèle 163** décompte annuel, on the same 31 December N+1 deadline.

There is **no series of forms named 100A–100L**. The official return is a **20-page modèle 100 F / 100 D** with letter-coded sheets (Signalétique, Enfants, E/R, NR, C/A, I, S, P, CM, L, D, EX, DS, CE, DD, RD and the page-20 recap). Separate annexes exist (190/210, 195, 700, 104, 102, 145, 180, 200, etc.).

There is **no “loi du 22 décembre 2024”** on the ACD legislation-2024 list. The resident-relevant December 2024 texts are dated **20 December 2024** (budget 2025 + LIR modifications + barème RGD), published in Mémorial A N° 558 / 563 / 589 / 590 of 23–24 December 2024. The housing package is the **loi du 22 mai 2024**. The “22.12” date in the brief most likely conflates the **loi du 22 décembre 2023** (2024 budget / four-bracket indexation) with the 20 December 2024 package.

For 2025 the tax scale was **re-indexed and reshaped** (first exempt bracket €13,230 class 1 / €26,460 classes 1a and 2; 2.5 index tranches on top of 2024; wide 39 % band). **CIS** became income-dependent (max €600, phase-out to €80,000). **CIM** rose to €3,504. **Bëllegen Akt** was raised to €40,000 per person. For **2026** the **barème is unchanged**; **CI-CO2 salarié** rises 192 → **216 €**; a **crédit d’impôt conjoncture** runs 1 June–31 December 2026 as a stand-in for one index tranche (barème to be indexed on 1 January 2027). A **single tax class U** was voted in late October 2026 to apply from **2028**, not 2025/2026.

## 2. Findings

### 2.1 Who must file modèle 100 (imposition par voie d’assiette)

**Fact.** Residents are *obligatoirement* subject to assiette (and therefore to Form 100) if they fall in one of the ACD categories below. Otherwise, salaried/pension residents may file **on request**, or may use modèle 163.

**Source (primary, current):** [ACD — Personnes physiques soumises à une imposition par voie d’assiette](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/i/impot_assiet_revenu.html), read 8 November 2026.

**Obligatory — residents (verbatim structure, not a paraphrase of the legal text):**

**I) Only income not subject to wage/interest/tantième withholding**, if taxable income exceeds **€12,438**.

**II) Everyone else, if:**
1. taxable income exceeds **€100,000**; **or**
2. in addition to withholding income, net income **not** subject to withholding exceeds **€600**; **or**
3. income includes remuneration exempt from withholding under art. 25 of the RGD 27 December 1974, or remuneration paid by foreign employers/caisses not subject to withholding under art. 28 of that RGD; **or**
4. holder of at least one **additional fiche de retenue à taux fixe**, if taxable income exceeds **€36,000** (classes 1 or 2) or **€30,000** (class 1a); **or**
5. net income subject to withholding on capitaux mobiliers exceeds **€1,500**; **or**
6. net income subject to withholding on tantièmes exceeds **€1,500**; **or**
7. married, not de facto separated, one spouse resident and the other non-resident, who wish or have opted jointly for collective assessment, provided the resident spouse realises at least **90 %** of the household’s professional income in Luxembourg; **or**
8. partners who wish or have opted jointly for collective assessment or individual assessment with reallocation; **or**
9. married persons who wish or have opted jointly for pure individual assessment or individual assessment with reallocation; **or**
10. holders of patrimony income and certain pensions subject to the contribution dépendance.

**On request — residents:** owner-occupier mortgage/financing costs; financing costs on rental buildings; certain dividends and bond interest (art. 97(1) 1 and 3 LIR); certain special indemnities; tantièmes net ≤ €1,500; foreign withholding on certain savings interest (art. 154(1) n° 3 LIR).

ACD may always **demand** a return (e.g. contribution dépendance base).

**Confidence:** Determination. Primary ACD page, current.

**App implication:** The €100,000 / €600 / €1,500 / €36,000 / €30,000 / €12,438 / 90 % thresholds are the filing-eligibility rules, not the tax-calculation rules. They must not be used as tax-scale breakpoints.

### 2.2 Deadline: 31 December of the following year; 2025 window 7 April – 31 December 2026

**Fact.** Whatever the channel (assistant électronique, MyGuichet PDF, post, drop-box), the return must be filed **at the latest on 31 December of the year following the tax year**. For tax year N: 31 December N+1. For 2025: **7 April 2026 to 31 December 2026**. ACD may impose shorter deadlines in individual courriers. Late or missing filing: supplément d’impôt, intérêt de retard, astreinte; refusal to file → **taxation d’office par voie d’estimation**.

**Sources:**
- [Guichet.lu — Déclaration pour l’impôt sur le revenu](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/fiscalite/declaration-impot-decompte/activite-professionnelle/declaration-revenus/declaration-impot.html), last modified 7 April 2026, read 8 November 2026: “Pour l’année d’imposition 2025, la période de remise de la déclaration pour l’impôt s’étend donc du 7 avril 2026 au 31 décembre 2026.”
- [Form 100 F 2025](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/formulaires/pers_physiques/2025/100f-2025.pdf), page 1/20: “La déclaration est à remettre remplie et signée pour le 31 décembre 2026 au bureau d’imposition compétent sous peine d’un supplément d’impôt pour dépôt tardif ou non-dépôt.”
- [ACD assiette page](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/i/impot_assiet_revenu.html): non-resident salariés not in assiette use modèle 163 by 31 December N+1; partner/married options must be filed by 31 December N+1.

**Modèle 163 vs 100.** Employees/pensioners **not** in obligatory assiette who only want to regularise RTS file **modèle 163** (décompte annuel), same 31 December N+1 deadline. They are not required to file 100.

**Confidence:** Determination.

**“100A–100L” verdict:** Not an official ACD naming. If the app or a third-party guide uses 100A–100L, it is a local invention or a confusion with the sheet letters (or with another country’s annex numbering). Mapping should use **official case numbers** (e.g. 201 enfants, 317 imposition collective, 701+ salaires, 1001+ location, 2001+ recap) not invented form IDs.

**Confidence:** Determination on structure. High on sheet content (direct PDF extract).

### 2.4 MyGuichet process and the “assisted declaration” (assistant électronique)

**Fact.** Since tax year 2021, eligible residents and non-residents can file via **MyGuichet.lu** with **LuxTrust or Luxembourg eID**. Email filing is **not** accepted. Paper, PDF-via-MyGuichet, drop-box and post remain available.

**Sources:**
- [ACD formulaires personnes physiques](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/formulaires/pers_physiques.html): “A partir de l’année d’imposition 2021, les contribuables résidents et non résidents éligibles peuvent déposer la déclaration en ligne via MyGuichet moyennant un produit LuxTrust.” Email not accepted.
- [Guichet.lu — assistant électronique](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/fiscalite/declaration-impot-decompte/activite-professionnelle/declaration-revenus/declaration-impot-electronique.html), last modified 7 April 2026, read 8 November 2026.

**Assistant électronique (the “assisted declaration”):**
- Target: persons under an ACD filing demand, **or** filing on request.
- **Since tax year 2024, all income categories** can be declared in the assistant.
- Automated ACD processing (no agent required); later control possible for **5 years**.
- Requires **13-digit matricule** + LuxTrust **or** eID. Foreign eIDAS tokens are **not** accepted for the signature.
- **Exclusions** (cannot use the assistant):
  - married more than once in the tax year;
  - changed country of residence in the tax year;
  - spouses/partners with different countries of residence in collective assessment (unless the non-resident couple already has an RTS rate on the fiche);
  - cannot be used **to request** (or after requesting) the mixed resident/non-resident collective option (90 % rule), pure individual assessment, or individual assessment with reallocation.
- If excluded: file modèle 100 PDF via MyGuichet PDF deposit, or by post.
- **Mandate:** professionnel mandatories use an espace professionnel; **contrat de mandat 101** must be attached, except for avocats. Death of a spouse: acte de décès may replace the mandat.
- **Joint filing signatures:** since AY 2024, **double signature**. If both have certificates: first signer sends the file to the other’s espace for the second signature. If one has no certificate: the certificate holder may sign for both **if** mandat 101 for the spouse is attached. For AY 2025 the method is unchanged; **the boxes to tick for the second signature changed** (guichet page, 7 April 2026).
- Optional: initiate by **XML** upload; **API pre-fill** via API Gateway étatique.
- Consent to exclusively automated processing is required and **revocable** (one spouse’s withdrawal is enough).

**Déclaration simple (2025).** In 2025, only taxpayers who had **only “salaire et pension”** and **no deduction beyond the minimum forfaitaire** received, in early March, a letter offering a **déclaration simple**; those who accepted received a pre-filled proposal in early May to check and validate (guichet.lu declaration-impot page). This is a separate, narrower assisted product from the assistant électronique.

**ACD 123easy!** is the official help site for filling the assistant (linked from guichet.lu).

**Confidence:** Determination on channels, exclusions, signatures, deadline. High.

### 2.5 Joint filing mechanics (résidents)

**Fact.** Married residents who are not de facto separated are **collectively assessed by default**. They may instead request, **jointly and irrevocably**, by 31 December N+1:
- **imposition collective** (art. 3 LIR) — one return, all worldwide income, solidary payment, **class 2 splitting**;
- **imposition individuelle pure** (art. 3ter (2) LIR);
- **imposition individuelle avec réallocation** (art. 3ter (3) LIR).

Partners (partenariat légal) may jointly request collective assessment **or** individual assessment with reallocation; the joint request is **non-revocable** and must be filed by 31 December N+1; **both partners must sign** the 100.

**Form 100 boxes (page 3, E/R):** 316 opening, 317 collective, 318 individuelle pure (fill 320–321), 319 individuelle avec réallocation (fill 322–323). If 316 and 317–319 are not filled, married persons are imposed **collectively** unless they expressed another choice before 31 December 2026.

**Class 2 arithmetic:** tax = **2 × Scale(RIA / 2)**. ACD worked example for 2025 (cited by financialservices.lu from the ACD splitting page): RIA €100,000 → Scale(€50,000) = €7,341 → class 2 tax **€14,682**.

**Class 1a:** widowed (after a 3-year class-2 continuation), persons with child modération d’impôt, and persons who have completed their **64th year** at the start of the tax year. Exempt bracket **€26,460** (double class 1). CIM is only for class 1a with a child in the ménage. The app plan’s “aged ≥ 65” wording is off by one year against art. 119 / ACD (“64e année accomplie”).

**Mixed resident / non-resident married couple:** collective option only if the resident spouse realises **≥ 90 %** of the household’s professional income in Luxembourg (ACD assiette item II.7; Form 100 NR page and 100F 2025 case 1101 area on the 100 also print the 90 % declaration).

**Non-residents** (for completeness, not the audit core): 9 months continuous salaried work in Luxembourg + the same €100k / €36k / €1,500 tests; or only indigène income without withholding; or tantièmes > €100,000; or assimilation option (art. 157bis/157ter).

**Confidence:** Determination. Primary ACD + Form 100.

The 2027 draft budget proposes **one additional index tranche from tax year 2027**, not 2026.

### 2.7 CIS / CI-CO2 (crédit d’impôt pour salariés) — 2025 and 2026

**Fact.** From AY 2025 the CIS is **income-dependent** (it was a flat €600 for all salary-earners before). The **2026 formula is identical**; only **CI-CO2 salarié** changes.

**Source (primary):** [ACD CIS 2025](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/c/credit-impot-salaries/cis2025.html) and [ACD CIS 2026](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/c/credit-impot-salaries/cis2026.html), both read 8 November 2026. Circulaires L.I.R. n° 152_ter/1, 154_quater/2, 154_quinquies/2 du 12 mars 2025 (ACD législation 2025).

**CIS, AY 2025 and AY 2026 (same):** on gross salary including art. 134 exempt salary:

- €936 to €11,265: CIS = **300 + (brut − 936) × 0,029** €/year  
- €11,266 to €40,000: CIS = **€600**/year  
- €40,001 to €79,999: CIS = **600 − (brut − 40,000) × 0,015** €/year  
- Below €936/year (€78/month, €3.12/day) or at/above **€80,000**/year (€6,667/month, €267/day): **no CIS**.

**CI-CO2 salarié AY 2025:** €936–€40,000: **€192**/year; €40,001–€79,999: **192 − (brut − 40,000) × 0,0048**; none below €936 or from €80,000.

**CI-CO2 salarié AY 2026:** same structure, **€216**/year in the flat zone; phase-out coefficient **0,0054**.

Conditions: art. 95 or 95a salary with Luxembourg taxing right; **once** per taxpayer; not cumulative with CII/CI-CO2 indépendant or CIP/CI-CO2 pensionné; compulsory affiliation to a Luxembourg or convention social-security scheme. Imputable and restituable in RTS; regularisable in 163 or 100.

**Confidence:** Determination.

### 2.8 CIM (crédit d’impôt monoparental) — 2025

**Fact.** From AY 2025 (loi du 20 décembre 2024, Mémorial A 589 — the same LIR-modifying law that carries CIS):

**Source (primary):** [ACD CIM 2025](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/c/cim/cim2025.html), read 8 November 2026.

- RIA **< €60,000**: CIM = **€3,504**  
- RIA **€60,000–€105,000**: CIM = **3,504 − (RIA − 60,000) × 0,0612**  
- RIA **> €105,000**: CIM = **€750**

Not a function of the number of children. Restitutable if CIM > cote. Only class **1a**, on request, at least one child triggering modération d’impôt. **Not** granted if both parents live with the child; **not** for married/class 2. Allocations to the child above **€2,712/year (€226/month)** reduce CIM by 50 % of the excess (alimentaire etc., not allocations familiales). Non-resident assimilated class 1a can obtain CIM **only** via modèle 100 (not 163), by 31 December N+1.

Form 100: request in Enfants sheet, case 228+.

**Confidence:** Determination.

### 2.9 Logement — Bëllegen Akt, prime jeune salarié, prime locative, abattement construction

#### 2.9.1 Bëllegen Akt

**Fact.** Credit on droits d’enregistrement et de transcription (normally **7 %** = 6 % + 1 %) for owner-occupied housing, **€40,000 per person**. Occupation 2 years (4 years for terrain à bâtir / en voie de construction). AED keeps a minimum of €100.

**Sources:**
- [Guichet.lu — Bëllegen Akt](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/citoyens/aides/logement-construction/aides-indirectes/credit-impot-actes-notaries.html), last modified 7 July 2025, read 8 November 2026.
- [Gouvernement — mesures adoptées logement et fiscalité 31 janvier 2024](https://gouvernement.lu/dam-assets/documents/actualites/2024/01/31-mesures-logement-fiscalite/mesures-adoptes-logement-et-fiscalit-31012024.pdf): 30,000 → 40,000 €.
- Loi du 22 mai 2024 (Mémorial A 219) raised the cap 30,000 → 40,000 for actes **1 January 2024 – 30 June 2025**.
- Loi du 27 juin 2025 (Mémorial A 271) **pérennise** the €40,000 (Chambre des députés séance 25 June 2025, projet 8540). Guichet page of 7 July 2025 already states the €40,000 limit as the current rule.
- A third-party 2026 article mentions a further move to €45,000; I did **not** find that on guichet.public.lu or AED today. Do not implement €45,000 without a Mémorial citation.

**Confidence:** Determination on €40,000 / 2-year occupation / 7 % backdrop. The €45,000 claim is **unverified** (opposed to the July 2025 guichet page).

#### 2.9.2 Prime jeune salarié (art. 115 n° 13d LIR) — new from 2025

**Source (primary):** [ACD — Prime jeune salarié](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/az/p/prime-jeune-salarie.html), read 8 November 2026. RGD 20 décembre 2024 (Mémorial A 592).

75 % of the prime is exempt if, at payment:
1. employee **< 30 years** at 1 January of the tax year;
2. **first CDI** with an employer established in Luxembourg (or foreign with LU établissement stable), contract signed from AY 2025;
3. first prime paid less than **5 years** ago at 1 January.

Annual exemptible maximum (full-time):
- brut ≤ €50,000: **€5,000**
- €50,000 < brut ≤ €75,000: **€3,750**
- €75,000 < brut ≤ €100,000: **€2,500**
- brut > €100,000: no exemption.

Changing employer loses the exemption.

#### 2.9.3 Prime locative (art. 115 n° 13c LIR)

Introduced by the loi du 22 mai 2024 (Mémorial A 219). RGD 22 mai 2024 (Mémorial A 220) sets calculation and exemption modalities. I did not open the RGD text today; the existence of the exemption article is certain from ACD’s 2024 legislation list and the housing-package titles. **Amount/formula: not extracted — do not guess.**
